About the Author

A passion for words.
A legacy of stories.

John Bemshima Benedict is a prolific and gifted writer with a deep passion for literature and creative expression. He graduated from the prestigious Benue State University, Makurdi, Nigeria, where he earned a Bachelor's Degree in Library and Information Science. He distinguished himself academically, graduating among the top students in his department.

He completed his secondary education at Mount La Salle College, Naka, where he laid the foundation for his academic and literary pursuits.

Driven by his love for creative writing, John has produced a variety of literary works spanning poetry, novels and dramatic plays. His writing reflects his creativity, insight and passion for storytelling.

His published works include Serving My Fatherland, Moonlight Stories with Grandma, Why I Married Naomi, and numerous inspiring poems.
